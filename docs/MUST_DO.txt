===============================================================================
 5. WRITE YOUR "WHY I BUILT THIS" PARAGRAPH
===============================================================================
 WHY : It is the first question in every single interview, and a weak answer
       here undoes a strong project. Write it down, say it out loud 5 times.

 THE ANSWER (adapt to your own words — do not read it robotically):

   "Small design and development agencies lose clients over communication, not
    over quality of work. Clients don't know what's happening on their project,
    so they keep asking for updates, and the team loses hours answering. I built
    ClientHub so each client gets a portal with live project progress, their
    invoices, and an AI-generated weekly summary that goes out automatically —
    so the update happens without anyone writing it. The interesting engineering
    problems were multi-tenant data isolation, verifying Razorpay webhook
    signatures so payment state can't be forged, and making the AI feature
    degrade gracefully when the API is unavailable."

 DONE WHEN :
   [ ] You can say this in under 45 seconds without reading it
